== S3-001 | intro | behaviour | ★
> The requirements from stages 1 and 2 continue to apply, with the additions below.
~ "Everything earlier still holds": every row of factory/ledger/stage-1.md (S1-001 … S1-208) and stage-2.md (S2-001 … S2-173) keeps applying, except where the "earlier rows changed" table says the text changed. run_all.py executes the stage-1 suite (p_*.py) and the stage-2 suite (q_*.py, and the browser probes u_*.py unless --no-ui) as part of stage 3.
@ run_all.py (stage-1 suite p_*.py + stage-2 suite q_*.py/u_*.py)
== S3-002 | intro | behaviour | -
> Numbered section references such as §5 and §7 refer to `stage-1.md`.
! informational
== S3-003 | intro | behaviour | -
> Users can request historical balances and paginated statements.
== S3-004 | intro | behaviour | ★
> Senders can correct eligible payments while preserving the original receipt.
~ Reading: every payment is eligible except settlement members and captures (S3-095, S3-098). Direct payments, request-paid payments and seeded payments are therefore correctable by their sender; the request stays `paid` with the same payment_id.
== S3-005 | intro | behaviour | ★
> Historical queries must support both the effective date of a payment and the information available at a specified time.
~ Bitemporal: `as_of` selects the effective time axis, `known_at` the recorded time axis. Probed on a grid of (as_of, known_at) pairs against an independent model of the specification.

== S3-006 | timestamps | time | ★
> Every payment's `created_at` is an RFC 3339 instant with an offset identifying when it moved money.
~ Seeded payments: the supplied instant (compared as an instant, any offset). API payments: within 5 minutes of the probe host's clock. Numeric ±HH:MM offset in responses (stage-1 reading S1-031).
== S3-007 | timestamps | behaviour | -
> Every endpoint returning a payment includes it.
~ POST /payments, POST /requests/{id}/pay, capture, settlement members, GET /activity and GET /statement entries.
== S3-008 | timestamps | behaviour | ★
> `GET /activity` retains its existing ordering by this field.
~ Newest first by created_at: seeded payments are ordered by their supplied created_at (not fixture order), and a correction that moves effective_at never reorders the feed.
== S3-009 | timestamps | time | ★
> Seeded payments may supply `created_at`; omission uses reset time, before subsequent API-created payments.
~ An omitted created_at is the reset instant (within the clock-skew allowance) and not later than the first API-created payment; it is later than payments seeded with explicit past instants.
== S3-010 | timestamps | error | ★
> A seeded `created_at` in the future gives `422 validation_failed` from `POST /_test/reset`, with no state change.
~ Probed with +1 h, +1 day and a far-future instant written with a non-UTC offset; earlier state (tokens, balances, payments) is intact afterwards; a past instant is accepted.
== S3-011 | timestamps | behaviour | ★
> A fixture's `balance` remains the balance after all seeded payments. Loading those payments must not change that balance.
~ /me balance equals the fixture balance exactly although seeded payments (with created_at) are loaded; a statement's closing_balance agrees.

== S3-012 | me | behaviour | -
> `as_of` is optional and is an RFC 3339 instant with an offset.
~ Reading: RFC 3339 grammar includes the `Z` designator, so `…Z` is accepted (own probe, so the reading can be contested in isolation).
== S3-013 | me | error | -
> Anything else — a naive local time, a bare date, an empty value — is 422 `validation_failed`.
~ Also garbage words, epoch digits, month 13, hour 25 (core list) and, in a separate probe, strict RFC 3339 grammar violations (no seconds, `+0000` without colon, Feb 30, offset +25:00).
== S3-014 | me | behaviour | ★
> Without temporal query parameters the response retains the existing money fields and reports current corrected values.
~ After corrections, plain GET /me shows the corrected balance (sender refunded/charged, receiver the mirror image); stage-2 fields balance/total/available/held stay.
== S3-015 | me | behaviour | ★
> With it, `balance` is the caller's balance as it stood at that instant: the balance after every payment of theirs with `created_at` at or before `as_of`, and before every payment after it.
~ With corrections the payment's selected revision decides (S3-071); without corrections effective_at = created_at so this text holds literally.
== S3-016 | me | time | ★
> A payment made at exactly `as_of` counts as having happened.
~ Probed with API payments (as_of = the created_at the service returned counts; one second earlier does not) and with seeded instants.
== S3-017 | me | behaviour | -
> An `as_of` at or after the latest payment returns the current balance.
~ Includes as_of far in the future.
== S3-018 | me | behaviour | ★
> An `as_of` before the earliest payment returns the opening balance — what the wallet held before anything moved.
== S3-019 | me | behaviour | ★
> The response carries `as_of` back, exactly as given.
~ Byte-for-byte echo: `+00:00`, `+02:00`, fractional seconds, `Z`.

== S3-020 | statement | behaviour | ★
> Both `from` and `to` are optional; `from` defaults to the opening of the wallet and `to` to now.
~ A statement without parameters lists every payment of the caller; closing_balance equals GET /me balance; from/to accept any RFC 3339 offset; invalid/empty from/to are 422 (reading of S1-075 and S3-074).
== S3-021 | statement | limit | ★
> `limit` and `offset` behave exactly as in `GET /requests`.
~ Default 50 / 1..200 / offset >= 0 / plain decimal digits (`1e2`, `+4`, `4.0` are 422) / unknown params ignored / has_more boundaries / offset past the end gives an empty page, has_more false, 200.
== S3-022 | statement | behaviour | ★
> Returns the payments the caller sent or received in the half-open window `[from, to)`, **oldest first**, each with the caller's balance immediately after it:
~ A payment at exactly `from` is inside, at exactly `to` outside; from == to gives an empty window.
== S3-023 | statement | behaviour | ★
> 1. Entries are ordered by `created_at` ascending, then payment `id` ascending for ties.
~ Superseded by S3-076 (effective_at) once corrections exist; identical without corrections. Ties among seeded payments use equal-length ids so string and numeric id order agree.
== S3-024 | statement | behaviour | ★
> 2. `opening_balance` is the balance immediately before `from`.
== S3-025 | statement | behaviour | ★
> `closing_balance` is the balance immediately before `to`.
== S3-026 | statement | behaviour | ★
> 3. `opening_balance` plus all `delta` values in the full window must equal `closing_balance`.
~ Verified on the whole window reassembled from pages, for every user, after corrections and zero-amount revisions.
== S3-027 | statement | behaviour | -
> A sent payment has a negative `delta`; a received payment has a positive `delta`.
~ delta == -amount for the sender, +amount for the receiver (selected amount); a zero-amount revision has delta 0.
== S3-028 | statement | behaviour | ★
> 4. Pagination must not change an entry's `balance_after` or the window's opening and closing balances. These values describe the full window regardless of `limit` and `offset`.
~ balance_after of an entry is a running sum from the full-window opening_balance, so on page 2 it does not restart from the page's first entry.
== S3-029 | statement | behaviour | ★
> Only payments sent or received by the caller appear in their statement, including when other payments are public.
== S3-030 | statement | behaviour | ★
> The activity-feed visibility rules do not apply to statements.
~ The caller's own private payments appear (as sender and as receiver); other people's public payments do not; an operator who is not a party to a settlement member does not see it.
== S3-031 | statement | behaviour | -
> { "opening_balance": 10000, "entries": [ { "payment": { "...": "..." }, "delta": -500, "balance_after": 9500 }, { "payment": { "...": "..." }, "delta": 1200, "balance_after": 10700 } ], "closing_balance": 10700, "has_more": false }
~ Shape: integers for the money fields, `entries` array of {payment, delta, balance_after, + revision fields of S3-077}, boolean has_more, string snapshot (S3-082). 401 without a valid bearer token (S1-070).

== S3-032 | effective | behaviour | -
> The service must distinguish **when money took effect** from **when it learned that fact**.
~ Effective time (effective_at, as_of, statement window) versus recorded time (recorded_at, known_at).
== S3-033 | effective | behaviour | -
> Every payment has a revision history.
~ GET /payments/{id}/revisions works for every payment: direct, request-paid, seeded, capture, settlement member.
== S3-034 | effective | behaviour | ★
> Revision 1 has `amount` as originally paid and `effective_at = recorded_at = created_at`.
~ Probed through the revisions endpoint and the statement entry fields, for API payments, seeded payments, settlement members and captures. Instants compared as instants.
== S3-035 | effective | behaviour | ★
> A seeded payment's supplied `created_at` is also its original recorded/effective time; omission uses reset time.
~ known_at earlier than a seeded payment's created_at excludes it; known_at at that instant includes it.
== S3-036 | effective | behaviour | ★
> Opening balances equal seeded ending balances minus the net effect of original seeded payments.
~ opening = fixture balance + sent - received over the seeded payments (original amounts). Hand-computed from fixtures.
== S3-037 | effective | behaviour | ★
> Corrections must not change those opening balances.
~ After corrections of seeded payments, as_of before everything and a statement with `to` before everything still report the same opening balance.
== S3-038 | effective | behaviour | -
> New accounts open at zero.
~ A signed-up user: statement opening_balance 0, as_of at any past instant 0 until the first payment arrives.
== S3-039 | effective | behaviour | -
> Seeded history is consistent and nonnegative.
! assumption about the inputs; the probes only ever seed consistent, nonnegative histories (checked by the fixture helper)

== S3-040 | corrections | idempotency | ★
> `POST /payments/{payment_id}/corrections` requires an idempotency key and the original sender.
~ Eighth idempotent write path: missing/empty key 400 missing_idempotency_key; key 1..255 characters (256 is 422); replay semantics of §7 apply (S3-118 … S3-120).
== S3-041 | corrections | error | ★
> An authenticated non-sender gets 403 `forbidden`; unknown payment gets 404.
~ Readings: the receiver gets exactly 403; a third party gets 403 on a public payment and 403 or 404 on a private one (not visible, S1-072); no token is 401 (S1-070).
== S3-042 | corrections | error | ★
> All fields are required.
~ Each of expected_revision, amount, effective_at, reason missing gives 422 validation_failed and changes nothing.
== S3-043 | corrections | limit | ★
> Revision is a positive integer;
~ 0, -1, 1.5 are 422. Wrong JSON types (string, boolean, null) are accepted as 400 or 422 (S1 §5 reserves 400 for wrong types, stage 3 says invalid input is 422).
== S3-044 | corrections | limit | ★
> amount is an integer 0..1000000000 (zero reverses the entire payment);
~ 0 and 1000000000 accepted, -1 and 1000000001 are 422; strings, booleans, null, arrays are 422 (S1-076); 400.0 and 4e2 are valid integral values (S1-036); zero reverses all of the payment.
== S3-045 | corrections | limit | ★
> reason is a string of 1..200 characters;
~ Characters are Unicode code points (S1-114): 200 emoji accepted, 201 rejected, "" rejected.
== S3-046 | corrections | time | ★
> effective time is an RFC 3339 instant not later than now.
~ A future effective_at is 422; an instant earlier than the payment's own created_at is allowed; an instant equal to the payment's created_at is allowed.
== S3-047 | corrections | error | -
> Invalid input is 422 `validation_failed`.
~ Also unparseable effective_at strings (naive, date only, empty). A non-object body and unparseable JSON stay 400 (S1-068).
== S3-048 | corrections | behaviour | ★
> Correction changes neither parties nor visibility.
~ Same from/to/visibility/note in the payment object everywhere; a private payment stays hidden from third parties; a public one stays visible.
== S3-049 | corrections | behaviour | -
> It appends an immutable revision, returning 201 with `payment_id`, `revision`, `amount`, `effective_at`, server-assigned `recorded_at`, and `reason`.
~ revision = previous + 1; recorded_at within the clock-skew allowance of the probe host; earlier revisions are unchanged by later ones.
== S3-050 | corrections | time | ★
> Recorded times for one payment strictly increase.
~ Ten corrections in quick succession (same second) still yield strictly increasing recorded_at, also greater than created_at of revision 1 (catches second-resolution clocks).
== S3-051 | corrections | error | -
> A stale expected revision gives 409 `stale_revision`.
~ Changes nothing and does not claim the idempotency key.
== S3-052 | corrections | idempotency | ★
> Successful replay returns that original revision with 200 even after newer revisions.
~ Replay body identical (as JSON) to the original 201 body; no new revision; no 409 stale_revision on replay.
== S3-053 | corrections | idempotency | -
> Different body with the same key is 409 `idempotency_key_reuse`.
~ Resolved before field validation and current-resource checks (S1-107): an invalid body with a claimed key is still 409 idempotency_key_reuse.
== S3-054 | corrections | behaviour | ★
> The difference from the previous amount moves between the **same two wallets** in the same atomic step.
~ Third parties are untouched; the debit/credit is never visible in one wallet only; a failed correction leaves no trace (S3-058).
== S3-055 | corrections | behaviour | ★
> Increasing the amount debits the original sender; decreasing it debits the original receiver.
~ Zero amount: the receiver gives back everything.
== S3-056 | corrections | error | ★
> A currently unaffordable debit gives 409 `insufficient_funds`.
~ Evaluated against `available` (stage 2): a receiver whose money is held by an open authorization cannot fund a decrease from the held part. Boundary: exactly the available amount succeeds.
== S3-057 | corrections | error | ★
> Otherwise, if any user's corrected balance is negative at any effective-time boundary, return 409 `historical_overdraft`.
~ Sender side (increase), receiver side (decrease), moving effective_at earlier or later, boundary: exactly zero is fine.
== S3-058 | corrections | error | ★
> Balances at a boundary include the combined effect of all movements at that instant.
~ A user who receives X and sends X at the same effective instant is at 0 there even if the send has the lower payment id; moving a payment onto that instant is accepted, one instant earlier is historical_overdraft. balance_after inside a tie follows the id order (transient negative values are legal there).
== S3-059 | corrections | error | ★
> Either failure preserves balances, revision history, statements and idempotency state.
~ Before/after comparison of /me, full statements, revisions and key reuse (the failed key is a first use afterwards).
== S3-060 | corrections | concurrency | ★
> The sum of balances must equal the seeded total in every historical view.
~ Checked for every (as_of, known_at) pair of the grid, including known_at before anything was recorded and as_of in the future.
== S3-061 | corrections | behaviour | ★
> The original payment and every original idempotent response remain unchanged.
~ Replaying the original POST /payments (and pay/settlement) key still gives 200 with the original amount; payment object in /activity keeps the original amount.
== S3-062 | corrections | behaviour | ★
> `GET /activity` continues to display the original payment; correction records are not new feed payments.
~ Same count, same ids, same order, original amounts, original created_at after any number of corrections.
== S3-063 | corrections | behaviour | ★
> `GET /payments/{payment_id}/revisions` returns `{"revisions": [...]}` in revision order, including revision 1 (`reason: ""`).
~ Reading: each element has the fields of the correction response (revision, amount, effective_at, recorded_at, reason; payment_id if present must match).
== S3-064 | corrections | behaviour | ★
> Only the two parties can read it; a third party gets 404 even for a public payment.
~ Sender and receiver 200; third party and a settlement operator who is not a party: exactly 404 not_found.
== S3-065 | corrections | error | -
> No token is 401.

== S3-066 | known_at | behaviour | -
> `GET /me` and `GET /statement` accept optional `known_at`, an RFC 3339 instant with offset.
== S3-067 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> For each payment, select its latest revision recorded **at or before** `known_at`; if none was yet recorded, that payment contributes nothing.
~ known_at exactly at a recorded_at includes that revision. A payment unknown at known_at contributes to neither party, so sums stay conserved.
== S3-068 | known_at | behaviour | -
> Omission means everything known when the read begins.
== S3-069 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> Then apply selected revisions according to their **effective** times.
~ Under known_at after a correction, the corrected effective_at decides whether the payment counts at a given as_of; under an earlier known_at the original one does.
== S3-070 | known_at | behaviour | -
> `as_of` retains its inclusive meaning; a statement retains its half-open window.
== S3-071 | known_at | time | ★
> Both query instants may be in the future.
~ as_of and known_at in the future are accepted (200) and behave as "now" for payments; for statements `to` in the future is accepted too.
== S3-072 | known_at | error | -
> Invalid/empty instants are 422.
~ Same value lists as S3-013, for known_at on /me and /statement.
== S3-073 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> Echo supplied `known_at` exactly.
~ /me echoes both as_of and known_at; the statement echoes known_at when supplied.
== S3-074 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> Statement ordering is now by selected `effective_at`, then payment id.
== S3-075 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> Each entry retains `payment`, `delta` and `balance_after`, and adds the selected `revision`, `effective_at` and `recorded_at`.
== S3-076 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> `payment.amount` is the selected amount for this statement.
~ The rest of the payment object (id, parties, visibility, note, created_at) is the original payment.
== S3-077 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> Zero-amount revisions still appear as entries with zero delta.
== S3-078 | known_at | behaviour | ★
> No correction is counted alongside the revision it replaces.
~ One entry per payment; deltas of a corrected payment are not summed with its original.
== S3-079 | known_at | behaviour | -
> With no corrections and no `known_at`, previous behavior is unchanged.

== S3-080 | snapshots | behaviour | ★
> Every first `GET /statement` response additionally returns an opaque `snapshot` token.
~ Present on every non-snapshot statement response, including empty windows and filtered ones.
== S3-081 | snapshots | behaviour | ★
> It freezes the caller's selected revisions, window, balances, entries and default `to` at that read.
~ Payments created after the read (later than the frozen default `to`) never appear in pages of that token.
== S3-082 | snapshots | behaviour | ★
> `GET /statement?snapshot=<token>&limit=...&offset=...` pages that exact result, even after payments or corrections.
~ Pages carry opening_balance, closing_balance, entries slice and has_more of the frozen result.
== S3-083 | snapshots | error | ★
> Only limit and offset may accompany a snapshot; supplying `from`, `to` or `known_at` with it gives 422 `validation_failed`.
~ Unrecognised parameters stay ignored (S3-087). `as_of` is not a statement parameter and is not probed.
== S3-084 | snapshots | error | ★
> Unknown token, another user's token, or a token from before reset gives 404 `not_found`.
~ Includes a token of the same user id after a reset to the same fixture (new login, old token).
== S3-085 | snapshots | time | -
> Tokens last until reset. No storage survival across container restarts is required.
~ A token is reusable any number of times (repeated and interleaved paging, minutes of use) until reset.
== S3-086 | snapshots | behaviour | ★
> Paging changes neither balances nor entries; the final partial page and offsets beyond the end must report `has_more` correctly.
== S3-087 | snapshots | behaviour | -
> Unrecognized query parameters remain ignored under stage 1's general rule.
== S3-088 | snapshots | behaviour | ★
> A correction may move a payment into or out of a statement window.
~ A correction changing effective_at moves the entry in/out of [from, to) and shifts opening_balance accordingly; an old snapshot still shows the old window content.
== S3-089 | snapshots | concurrency | ★
> Existing snapshots remain unchanged during concurrent payments or corrections.
~ Reader threads page a token continuously during a storm of payments and corrections; every page equals the first read.
== S3-090 | snapshots | concurrency | ★
> Concurrent corrections using the same expected revision cannot both succeed.
~ Burst with different keys: exactly one 201, the rest 409 stale_revision, exactly one new revision. Burst with one key: one 201, the rest 200 with the same body.

== S3-097 | holds | behaviour | ★

~ as_of exactly at creation counts (inclusive).
== S3-099 | holds | time | ★

== S3-100 | holds | time | ★

== S3-101 | holds | time | ★

== S3-102 | holds | time | ★

== S3-105 | holds | time | ★

== S3-106 | holds | behaviour | ★

== S3-107 | holds | behaviour | ★
